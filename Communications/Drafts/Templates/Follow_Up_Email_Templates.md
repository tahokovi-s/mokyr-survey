# Follow-Up Email Templates -- Mokyr Academic Genealogy Survey

These templates are for follow-up reminders to non-respondents across survey waves. All emails are sent from Ran Abramitzky. Personalization variables are listed after each template.

---

## Template 1: Gen 1 Personal Follow-Up (Direct Mokyr Advisees)

**When to use:** For people who were directly advised by Joel Mokyr (Gen 1) and have been contacted 2-4 times without responding. These are the highest-priority non-respondents.

**Subject:** Joel Mokyr intellectual genealogy -- your response would mean a lot

**Body:**

> Dear [FIRST_NAME],
>
> I hope this finds you well. I'm writing one more time about the Joel Mokyr Academic Genealogy Survey. We are documenting Joel's intellectual family tree -- his students, their students, and so on -- and as one of Joel's direct advisees, your participation is especially valuable.
>
> The survey is brief (about 5 minutes) and asks about your academic background, career, and any PhD students you may have supervised. Your entry helps us trace the full reach of Joel's mentorship across generations.
>
> I understand you're busy, and I appreciate your considering this. If you have a few minutes, the survey is here:
>
> [SURVEY_LINK]
>
> Thank you, and all the best.
>
> Ran Abramitzky
> Stanford University

**Personalization variables:**
- `[FIRST_NAME]` -- Recipient's first name
- `[SURVEY_LINK]` -- Qualtrics survey URL

>

> Ran Abramitzky
> Stanford University

**Personalization variables:**
- `[FIRST_NAME]` -- Recipient's first name

## General Notes

- All emails should be sent from Ran Abramitzky's email address.
- The survey link is a Qualtrics anonymous link (same for all recipients) or an individual link if using Qualtrics contact-list distribution.
- For BCC mass sends, use the generic versions (without `[ADVISOR_NAME]`). For targeted individual follow-ups, personalize fully.
- Do not send more than one follow-up per person per 2-week period.
- If a recipient has started but not finished the survey (partial response), consider a more targeted note: "I noticed you started the survey -- if you have a few more minutes to finish it, that would be great."
